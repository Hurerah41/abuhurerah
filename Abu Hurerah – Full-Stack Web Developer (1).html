MediQueue, inside

The problem, the people it serves, what it does, and what I built.
↗View on GitHub

The problem

In many clinics and hospitals, patients face long waiting times, manual appointment processes and trouble finding nearby doctors. MediQueue connects patients with healthcare providers through one central platform.

Who it's for

  • Patients looking for nearby doctors and healthcare services
  • Doctors managing appointments and patient queues
  • Clinics and hospitals managing their operations
  • Administrators overseeing the whole platform

Built with

PHPLaravelMySQLTailwind CSSHTML / CSS / JSSMTP email

My role

  • Backend development with Laravel and PHP
  • Designed and managed MySQL database structures
  • Built modules for patients, doctors, providers and admins
  • Implemented booking and queue management workflows
  • Built Tailwind interfaces and role-based dashboards

Payment gateway integration and AI-powered assistance are planned as future enhancements.
